# Content
- What the site promises (countries, features, prices) must match the real product.
- Describe what the product does, not what users might wrongly assume it does. For example, "tells you what to invoice", not "invoices for you".
- Recheck published figures against their source and update them everywhere they appear (page, CV, social card).
- Legal: get one review of the base-language version, then translate it. Consumer law in some countries (for example Brazil and Portugal) expects contracts in the local language.

# Less per screen, especially on phones
- The first phone screen shows only: an optional short announcement, the headline, one short paragraph and at most two buttons.
- Secondary controls (the header CTA, a floating contact button, back-to-top) appear after the visitor scrolls past the hero. Hide them with `display: none`, so they don't hold space.
- One compact contact modal (name, email, message) beats loose email addresses scattered across the page.
- Show prices for the visitor's country automatically, with no region picker.

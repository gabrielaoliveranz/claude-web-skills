# Cards and carousels
- Cards in a row share the same height and align their inner parts (CSS subgrid). Nothing jumps on tap.
- On phones and tablets, turn rows into carousels:
  - arrows sit beside the dots, never on top of the cards;
  - the arrow you can't use fades out;
  - gentle autoplay that stops on touch.
- Button groups stay centred; on tablet split them evenly (for example 4 + 3).

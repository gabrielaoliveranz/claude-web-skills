# Readability and accessibility
- Contrast of at least 4.5:1, including hover and focus states, checked automatically.
- Readable labels on dark sections and a proper heading order.
- Keep phrases that shouldn't break together with a non-breaking space (`New Zealand`).
- Body text around 65 characters wide; `text-wrap: balance` on headings.
- Plain, confident copy with no jargon. Describe what the product does, looking forward.
